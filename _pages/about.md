I am a PhD student in the [Cryptology and Data Security Group](https://crypto.unibe.ch/) at the University of Bern. My research lies at the intersection of cryptography, privacy, and distributed systems, with a particular interest in digital currencies and decentralized infrastructure.

I develop formal models to understand the guarantees systems provide and design protocols with provable security. I am interested in how privacy interacts with scalability, synchronization, and resilience, and in building systems that protect users while remaining practical to operate. More broadly, my interests include privacy-enhancing technologies and censorship-resistant systems.

My research also has an interdisciplinary dimension. In collaboration with legal researchers, I explore how cryptographic systems can reconcile individual privacy with accountability and regulatory oversight. I am particularly interested in using cryptography to make these guarantees enforceable through technical mechanisms and reduce reliance on institutional trust.
